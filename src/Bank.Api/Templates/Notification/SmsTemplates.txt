SMS TEMPLATE DEFINITIONS
========================

Template ID: 2fa_token
Message: Your Bank verification code is: {Token}. Valid for {ExpiryMinutes} minutes.
---

Template ID: password_reset
Message: Your password reset code is: {ResetCode}. Valid for {ExpiryMinutes} minutes. Never share this code.
---

Template ID: transaction_alert
Message: Transaction alert: ${Amount} {TransactionType} from account {AccountLast4} on {TransactionDate}. If not you, contact support.
---

Template ID: payment_confirmation
Message: Bill payment confirmed: ${Amount} to {BillerName} on {PaymentDate}. Reference: {PaymentReference}
---

Template ID: account_locked
Message: Your account has been locked due to security concerns. Contact support at 1-800-BANK-123 to unlock your account.
---

Template ID: suspicious_activity
Message: Suspicious activity detected on your account. Please verify your recent transactions or reset your password immediately.
---

Template ID: deposit_maturity_notice
Message: Your fixed deposit (Account: {DepositNumber}) matures on {MaturityDate}. Action required: Choose renewal or withdrawal.
---

Template ID: low_balance_alert
Message: Balance alert: Your account balance is now ${CurrentBalance}. Minimum balance is ${MinimumBalance}.
---
